Privacy policy

How OCR Image Reader handles your information

Andreas Express (libandreas) develops OCR Image Reader. This policy explains how OCR Image Reader handles your data.

Last updated: 19 November 2026.

Overview

When you choose Extract Text, Chrome asks you to select and share a tab, window or screen. OCR Image Reader takes a still image of that source and stops the capture stream. The image may include personal information visible in the source you selected, including areas outside the region you later choose for recognition.

You then select an area to read. Image recognition runs locally on your device using resources included with the extension. Screen images and recognized text are not uploaded to us or an online OCR service. The extension does not monitor browsing activity or record microphone audio.

Information the app uses

  • A screenshot of the tab, window, or screen you choose to share
  • A temporary window identifier used to open the recognition page
  • The area you select and the text recognized in that area

OCR Image Reader processes all of this information on your device. It copies the result to your clipboard. If you have enabled clipboard sync, your device may copy that text to your other devices.

Temporary storage and clipboard

The screenshot and a browser window identifier are held temporarily in browser session storage to open the recognition page. That page removes the stored capture when it reads it and keeps a working copy while open. An unread capture can remain until the browser session ends.

Recognized text is written automatically to your clipboard. OCR Image Reader does not read your existing clipboard contents or keep a history of recognized text. Clipboard history and synchronization are controlled by your device and services you enable.

Saved settings

The extension saves your language preference and may retain license keys, license status, verification times and feature-access records in browser storage. These settings can sync through your Google account when Chrome sync is enabled. Screenshots are not placed in Chrome sync by the extension. Recognition resources may be cached locally by the browser.

Licenses and external services

The current free version hides the license controls, but a previously saved license may still be checked when you open the popup. For a Gumroad license, the extension sends the license key and product identifier directly to Gumroad over HTTPS. Gumroad may return a purchase email and validation status. These requests do not include screenshots or recognized text. Supported signed licenses are verified locally.

Gumroad receives connection information such as your IP address. Purchases and payment details are handled by Gumroad and its payment providers under Gumroad's privacy policy. Chrome synchronization is governed by Google's privacy policy.

OCR Image Reader does not send usage statistics or automatic error reports. Diagnostic messages stay in the local console.

If you contact us for support, we use the message, contact details, and attachments you choose to send to answer your request.

Use and protection of information

OCR Image Reader uses information for text recognition, copying results, saving preferences and managing license access. We do not sell user data, use it for advertising or credit decisions, or use captured content to train AI models. We do not receive your OCR content unless you choose to send it to us for support.

Our handling of user data, including information obtained through Google APIs, complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy and its Limited Use requirements. Data use and transfers are limited to the purposes disclosed here. Support content is accessed only to address your request, with your consent, or where necessary for security or legal obligations.

License requests use HTTPS. Local information is held in your browser profile or memory and relies on your browser and device protections.

Your choices and deletion

You can cancel Chrome's sharing prompt, choose a narrower capture source, or close the recognition page. Change the saved language in Settings. Clear the extension's storage or uninstall it to remove its stored data, and manage synced settings through Chrome. Clear clipboard history separately if you want to remove copied text.

Deleting the extension does not remove text you have pasted elsewhere or information held by Gumroad. Contact the relevant provider for those records. For information you sent us for support, contact us to request access or deletion. We retain support information only as needed to handle the request or meet legal obligations.

Changes and contact

We update this policy when our data practices change. For privacy questions or requests, use the contact form or the developer email below.
